The Willow Bookshop opens Tuesday through Saturday, from 10:00 to 18:00. It is closed on Sundays and Mondays.

Customers can collect reserved books at the front counter. Bring the reservation number and collect within seven days of the notification email.

The bookshop hosts a reading group on the first Wednesday of each month at 17:00. Admission is free, but seats must be reserved by email.
