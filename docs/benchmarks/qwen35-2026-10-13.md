# Qwen3.5-0.8B generation, CPU: uncontrolled development run, 2026-10-13

> **Uncontrolled development numbers, not G5 evidence.** One brief run on a shared workstation while other agents' builds, tests, soak runs and a GPU job ran (load average 14 to 21 on 12 logical CPUs). A smoke run earlier the same day measured the same short prompt at 2.9 decode tokens/s and the long prompt's 8 decode steps at 11 tokens/s, so contention, not the model, sets these numbers. They show that the harness works and what it records; they must not be compared with a budget or quoted as performance. The G5 budget (including the plan's proposed 95% of matched native decode throughput) is a maintainer decision (production-readiness plan, M2), and G5 needs a controlled machine (M6). ## Quality attached to the timings

Preliminary sweep: all 22 reference prompts from their prompt ids, greedy for 16 tokens: 22/22 equal to the reference ids; the 21 prompts without tools rendered through the owned chat template and tokenizer: 21/21 equal to the reference text and prompt ids. Every timed series was then regenerated in full and compared exactly with the reference up to its `max_new_tokens` (64, 32 or 8): all equal.
