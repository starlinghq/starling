Modifications made by StarlingHq (Apache-2.0, section 4(b)):

  embeddinggemma2-vision-fp32.onnx
  - Only the image path is included: the vision tower (patch embedder with its x and y position tables, 16 encoder
    layers), the 3x3 average pooling to soft tokens with the sqrt(768) scale, and embed_vision (RMSNorm without scale,
    Linear 768 to 512) of EmbeddingGemma2Model. The text and audio towers are not included.
  - The forward pass was restated for export (model-pipeline/reference/embeddinggemma2/eg2_vision.py, VisionPath): a
    fixed pooling kernel of 3, an equality against arange in place of one_hot, an additive float32 attention mask for
    padding patches, and fixed-shape outputs (soft_tokens [batch, patches / 9, 512], pooled [batch, patches / 9, 768],
    soft_token_mask [batch, patches / 9]); upstream's stripping of padding soft tokens is left to the caller.
  - The weights were converted from bfloat16 to float32 and exported to ONNX (opset 20) with the PyTorch dynamo
    exporter (torch 2.14.1, transformers 5.19.0, onnx 1.23.1, onnxscript 0.7.2).

  embeddinggemma2-text-mm-fp32.onnx
  - The released text-path graph of release embeddinggemma-2-onnx-r1 (embeddinggemma2-text-fp32.onnx, SHA-256
    02cc7ecccbfcb29d812eb104e4c6ea6c599bc87e94f01082606f6a11f0252d11; its own notice lists its modifications) with two
    more inputs, multimodal_embeds (float32, [batch, sequence, 512]) and multimodal_mask (bool, [batch, sequence]), and
    five more nodes: where the mask is set, the soft token replaces the scaled token embedding, and its per-layer
    embedding projection (same weights) replaces the token's. No weight was changed or added; with an all-false mask
    the graph gives the released graph's outputs.

  Both files
  - Exporter metadata and doc strings were removed so the files are reproducible; the weights are stored inline.
  - Image decoding, the image processor (resize, rescale, patches), the placement of soft tokens, mean pooling, L2
    normalization, prompts and Matryoshka truncation are not part of the graphs.
