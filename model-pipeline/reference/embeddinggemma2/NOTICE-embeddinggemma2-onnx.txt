Modifications made by StarlingHq (Apache-2.0, section 4(b)):

  - Only the text path is included: the token embedding, the per-layer embeddings, the 24 encoder layers, the final
    norm and the 512-to-768 embedding_projection of EmbeddingGemma2TextModel. The vision and audio towers are not
    included; image, audio and video inputs are not supported.
  - The weights were converted from bfloat16 to float32 and exported to ONNX (opset 20) with the PyTorch dynamo
    exporter (torch 2.14.1, transformers 5.19.0, onnx 1.23.1, onnxscript 0.7.2). The attention masks are built in the
    graph from the 2-D attention mask as additive float32 masks (bidirectional; sliding window |q - k| <= 512 on the
    sliding layers). Outputs: last_hidden_state (512) and token_embeddings (768).
  - Exporter metadata and doc strings were removed so the file is reproducible; the weights are stored inline.
  - Mean pooling, L2 normalization, prompts and Matryoshka truncation are not part of the graph.
