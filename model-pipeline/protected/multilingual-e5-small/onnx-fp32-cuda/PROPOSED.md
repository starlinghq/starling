Decisions for the reviewer:

1. The profile pins `use_tf32: 0`. ORT 1.30.0's CUDA EP default (`use_tf32` 1) moves hidden states by up to 1.54e-3 and embeddings by up to 7.4e-5; with TF32 off, CUDA is within 2.1e-6 (hidden) and 1.9e-7 (embedding) of the reference, the same order as ORT CPU. Accept TF32 off as part of the profile identity, or define a separate TF32 profile.
2. ORT assigns 2 of 351 optimized nodes (a `Gather` and an `Unsqueeze` on the shape path) to the CPU EP by design. Accept that as the expected placement, or require a stricter check.
3. No `tolerances.yaml` is proposed. Observed errors are in `parity.json` and `docs/m0/e5-gpu.md`.
4. One GPU and one driver were tested, shared with other jobs during the run. Linux x64 CUDA is marked experimental in the manifest; Windows CUDA is untested.
